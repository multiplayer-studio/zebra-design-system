Zebra Design System

An enterprise utility language: calm, precise and built for frontline workers who scan screens quickly, often on rugged handhelds, with gloves on or in bad light. Cool-neutral greys, one confident Zebra Blue, hairline borders and IBM Plex Sans.

Colour

Semantic colour tokens. Values shown are live: switch Dark or High contrast in the header and every swatch re-resolves through the same token name.

Typography

IBM Plex Sans throughout. Display sizes use the light weight; titles and labels use medium. Headings h1–h6 alias headline-large … title-small.

Spacing, shape & depth

Everything snaps to the 4px mini-unit. Corners are barely softened by default; depth comes from hairline borders and very soft cool-grey shadows.
